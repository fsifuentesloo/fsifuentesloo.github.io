### 1. Global Bulk Payment Automation & Control Framework
* **Account Scope:** Citi-US-USD Corporate Account
* **Volume & Scale:** Executed **40 cross-border/domestic transactions** totaling **~$8.5M USD** across global subsidiaries in a single automated batch run.
* **Objective:** Eliminate manual transaction entry, secure cash outbound workflows, and standardize batch processing.

#### ⚠️ The Challenge
* **Manual Bottlenecks:** Treasury operations required the manual execution of dozens of individual high-value transaction requests monthly across dozens of international jurisdictions.
* **Operational Risk:** Manual data entry into banking portals increased exposure to human error, typos, or processing delays on significant capital flows ($8.5M+ USD).
* **Audit & Traceability Gaps:** Fragmented transactional trails made end-to-end tracing slow and labor-intensive.

#### 🛠️ The Solution
* **Macro-Based Data Consolidation:** Built an Excel VBA macro that automatically pulls, cleans, and formats payment requests from a standardized treasury intake template.
* **Built-in Account Validation:** Added automated validation rules to check beneficiary bank details, routing codes, and required fields before file generation.
* **Bank File Output:** Automated the creation of bank-formatted batch files ready for direct upload into the Citi payment portal.

#### 📈 Business Impact
* **Efficiency:** Transformed the execution of **40 individual payments totalling $8.5M USD** from hours of manual portal entry into an automated batch upload completed in minutes.
* **Risk Mitigation:** Achieved a zero operational error rate on major international disbursements by enforcing automated data validation on all beneficiary records prior to bank execution.
* **Strengthened Controls:** Standardized the workflow, providing internal and external auditors with an exact, auditable trace from source template to bank settlement.

### 2. Multi-Entity Transaction & Refund Tracking Dashboard
* **Scope:** 40 to 60 Global Subsidiaries
* **Objective:** Partnered with IT to design and deploy a centralized dashboard to standardize incoming banking requests (customer refunds and internal transfers) and eliminate email processing bottlenecks.
  
#### ⚠️ The Challenge
* **Unstructured Email Intake:** Treasury received high volumes of transaction requests across 40–60 subsidiaries via unstructured emails with inconsistent formats and missing data fields.
* **Operational Friction:** Manually sorting through fragmented email threads to verify and track pending requests created processing bottlenecks and delayed execution.
* 
#### 🛠️ The Solution
* **Cross-Functional Requirement Design:** Defined functional specifications for IT to engineer a centralized treasury dashboard tailored to multi-entity funding workflows.
* **Standardized Intake Architecture:** Co-designed structured input fields and validation rules to ensure local entities submit complete, verified banking details upfront.
* **Centralized Status Tracking:** Implemented real-time visibility features to monitor request lifecycles (pending, active, completed) across all operating entities.

#### 📈 Business Impact
* **Eliminated Email Bottlenecks:** Replaced fragmented email threads with a single, IT-supported intake platform, drastically reducing review time.
* **Data Accuracy:** Enforced mandatory field inputs at submission, preventing delays caused by incomplete or incorrect banking information.
* **Cross-Department Alignment:** Established a clear, real-time tracking mechanism connecting corporate treasury, IT, local subsidiaries, and accounting.
